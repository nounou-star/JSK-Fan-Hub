The Canaries' Epic Journey

Since 1946, a club etched in the heart of Algeria

⚜️ 1946 – Birth of the club

Founded in Tizi Ouzou under the name JS Kabylie, it quickly became a cultural and sporting symbol.

🏅 1973 – First Algerian League title

The beginning of a national dynasty, as the club imposed its attacking style.

🌍 1981 – CAF Champions League 🏆

First continental title, a legendary victory against AS Vita Club. Second triumph in 1990.

🏆 1995 – CAF Cup

JSK added another African trophy to its honours, becoming one of the continent’s most successful clubs.

🇩🇿 14× Algerian Champions

National record with 14 league titles, the most successful club in the country.
